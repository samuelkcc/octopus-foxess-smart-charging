Before you start

Current deployment workflow: clean installation, Wi-Fi and IP verification, account saving, local Dashboard, and OTA.

Example rules: Follow Smart Dispatch is on. Weekly charging runs from 23:30 to 05:30 every day. Price rules and weekly discharge are off. Optional charge-target switches are off. Adapt these examples to your installation.

Enable FoxESS Mode Scheduler. Set Remaining Time Mode to Self-Use.

The ESP32 checks updates after startup, then every 24 hours. Automatic installation is never enabled.

All account details and readings are demonstration data. No real board was erased or inverter commanded for this recording.
